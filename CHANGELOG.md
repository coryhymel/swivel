## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-27

First public release.

### Added
- Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive for several Google accounts in one window.
- One persistent, isolated session per account: sign in once, stay signed in.
- Account rail with colors, unread badges and the account email; rename,
  recolor and remove accounts.
- Keyboard shortcuts for switching accounts (Ctrl+1…9, Ctrl+Tab) and services (Alt+1/2/3).
- Links from Gmail and Calendar open in your default browser.
- Cookie encryption with Windows DPAPI, a strict permission allowlist and
  hardened Electron fuses.
- Windows installer (per-user, no admin rights) with automatic updates from
  GitHub Releases.
